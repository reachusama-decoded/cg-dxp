# Data Creators — instructor pack

[Data Creators › instructor pack — instructor pack]{.breadcrumb}

Instructor-only material for the five-day Data Creators programme. The learner-facing material (anchor notebooks, activity cards, demos, the running example, prompts and diagrams) is in `../courseware/`, which can be shared with learners as it is; nothing in this folder goes on the projector.

Start with [00-week/start-here.md](00-week/start-here.md).

| Folder | What is in it |
|---|---|
| `00-week/` | The week in one page, the pre-flight checklist, the LLM guide (Claude on this laptop, Copilot in the room, prompts to share), risks and fallbacks. Each as `.md`, `.docx` and `.pdf`. |
| `day1-design/` … `day5-capstone/` | Per day: `run-sheet.md` and the printable `run-sheet.docx` (the timed companion to the anchor, in the notebook's order: purpose, what is on screen, what to say, the whiteboard scripts with the answers rooms give, the demo steps with their coaching, fallbacks, transitions and flex); `dayN_prep-guide.md` and `.docx` (an hour's reading to be workshop-ready: every concept of the day in depth, teaching notes, demo and challenge mechanics, a readiness self-test and the evening-before checklist). Every document also has a `.pdf` beside it, the version to print or send. |

Everything per day is generated by `../research/scripts/notebooks/build_all.py` from the day modules and, for the Word files, by a copy of CG Core's lesson-plan builder in `../research/scripts/docx/` (needs `node` and `pandoc`; `--no-docx` skips it). The prep guides are written by hand as Markdown and only built. The PDFs are printed from the Markdown by `../research/scripts/docx/md_to_pdf.py` (pandoc plus headless Chrome with a house print stylesheet): `build_all.py --week --pdf` rebuilds all of them.
