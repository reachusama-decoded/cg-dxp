# My product requirements document

Complete this worksheet in phase 0 and extend it with your team in phase 2. Each `AUTHOR:` line marks a section for you to write; replace it with your reviewed requirements.

## Consumer and problem
AUTHOR: Who is your internal consumer? What question do they need to answer, and how will they use the output?

## Scope and exclusions
AUTHOR: Which records belong in phase 1 and phase 2? Which fields are unknown? Which claims are outside this dataset’s scope?

## Three consumer enquiries
AUTHOR: Describe product lookup, the published fund-expense filter and performance comparison. What should the consumer see when information is missing or incompatible?

## Acceptance and failure behavior
AUTHOR: What does one output row represent? Which IDs must be present? State the units, date rules and comparison rules. What must happen when input or quality checks fail?

## Ownership and changes
AUTHOR: Name who builds the data (Data Creator), agrees scope/version (Data Product Manager), reviews meaning/quality (Data Steward) and uses the output (consumer). How will they review a change?
