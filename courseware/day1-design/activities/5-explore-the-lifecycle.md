# Activity 5 — Explore the lifecycle

*Day 1 · Individual, then tables · 15 minutes*

Ask Catalyst about itself: the phases of the data product lifecycle at CG, what the build phase requires, and which of those requirements have a skill. Ten minutes asking, five minutes for each table to share one correction and the evidence for it.

**Produce**

- The nine phases, in order
- The build-phase prerequisites (PRD, data contract, data model, transformation spec) and the skill for each
- One thing Catalyst got wrong or vague, with the guidance that corrects it

**Debrief questions**

- Which phase are today's artefacts in?
- What would you ask Catalyst before using a skill tomorrow?

**Prompts to try.** "What are the phases of the data product lifecycle at Capital Group?" · "What does the build phase require, and which requirements have a skill?" · "What do I need to know before I use the data-contract skill?"
