# Activity 1 — Introductions and pain points

*Day 1 · Tables · 15 minutes*

Five minutes: go round the table with team, role and one hobby, and find a hobby you share. Ten minutes: list the data products you work on or depend on, and what is annoying about each one. Write them on the paper; a note taker keeps the list.

**Produce**

- A written list of the table's data products (one line each: name, who uses it, what for)
- For each, the sharpest pain point
- A group name

**Debrief questions**

- Which products does the table rely on but not trust, and why?
- Who makes a decision with each one?
