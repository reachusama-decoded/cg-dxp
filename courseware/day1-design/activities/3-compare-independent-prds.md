# Activity 3 — Compare independent PRDs

*Day 1 · Pairs · 10 minutes*

Both of you describe the same product to Catalyst, separately and without showing each other, and generate a PRD each. Then compare scope, consumers, requirements and assumptions. Find one or two differences that matter.

**Produce**

- Two PRDs for one product
- One or two differences that matter, and which input caused them
- One correction or piece of context each of you would add

**Finished early?** Swap one requirement with another pair: is it precise enough to check? Bring the disagreement to the debrief.

**Debrief questions**

- Where did the detail of the input change the output?
- What did the model invent: a field, an owner, an approval, a revision history?

**How to generate.** In Copilot Chat: `/ai-catalyst product-requirements-document` followed by your description (what the product is, who consumes it, the source, the decision it supports). If `/ai-catalyst` does not appear, the plugin is not enabled: see `GETTING_STARTED.md` in the training repository.
