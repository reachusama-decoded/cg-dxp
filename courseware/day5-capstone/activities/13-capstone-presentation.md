# Activity 13 — Capstone presentation

*Day 5 · One person per table · 3 minutes*

Two to three minutes: what you built and for whom, what was hard and what was easy, one finding in the data, one trust question you answered and how, and what you would do next. If you used Catalyst or the design documents, show one. If senior leaders are in the room, say where the paved path and Catalyst helped.

**Produce**

- The outputs you built, with one number you checked independently
- The hardest step, and the finding
- One trust question and its answer

**Debrief questions**

- Which teams picked the same outputs, and did their numbers agree?
- What would you tell the next cohort before they start?
