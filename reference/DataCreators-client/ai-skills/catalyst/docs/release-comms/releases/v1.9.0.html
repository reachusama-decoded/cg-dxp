v1.9.0 — GA: End-to-End Data Product Lifecycle

June 29, 2026

This is the AI Catalyst GA release. Every required phase to take a data product end-to-end through the Data Product Lifecycle — from intake and discovery, through design, build, validate, and publish — is now supported by a live orchestrator. With the new /deploy-dab skill closing the build inner loop, you can now create, validate, and deploy a data product without leaving Copilot Chat.

What’s new in v1.9.0:

  • /deploy-dab — new skill that deploys your Databricks Asset Bundle to your own dev space, closing the Phase 4 build-and-deploy inner loop
  • DAG standards, ratified — /generate-dag and /validate-dag now align to the published RFD 0011 Airflow DAG standard
  • Reliability fixes — Confluence publishing and the behavior-test harness are more dependable

1. /deploy-dab — Deploy Your Bundle to Your Dev Space

After /setup-databricks-asset-bundle authors your bundle and /validate-dbt gives it the green light, /deploy-dab pushes it to your own dev space — one data product per run. It validates the bundle, deploys it, and hands you direct links to the deployed job and pipeline. It can also tear a local deploy back down on request, behind an explicit confirmation.

This closes the Phase 4 inner loop: build → validate → deploy, all in Copilot Chat. Shared and production deploys remain CI/CD’s job — this is your personal dev iteration loop.

2. DAG Standards, Ratified (RFD 0011)

The Airflow DAG standard is now published, and both /generate-dag and /validate-dag are aligned to it. DAGs you generate and the compliance reports you get back now cite the same ratified rule set, so what you build and what you audit against stay in lockstep.

If you author or review Airflow DAGs, your generated scaffolding and audit findings are now grounded in the published standard.

What’s next

With the core create-to-publish path now GA, our focus shifts to maturing the back half of the lifecycle and sharpening what’s already shipped:

  • Operate & Evolve phases — promoting today’s leaf skills (runbooks, incident logs, deprecation notices, usage analytics) into full phase orchestrators, so the last two lifecycle phases match the rest
  • Feedback-driven refinement — folding your catalyst-feedback into improvements on the skills you already use every day

A note on platforms

This GA release is validated primarily on macOS. Windows users can use Catalyst, but may hit gaps in the technical prerequisites for some skills — for example, available laptop memory and compute, local tooling setup, and environment configuration. We’re actively closing those gaps — if you’re on Windows and something doesn’t work, please tell us via catalyst-feedback.

Also shipped

  • Confluence publishing fix — corrected the page-update URL so automated Confluence updates (e.g. triage pages) publish reliably
  • Behavior-test harness — added the stdout_contains structural check for more precise skill testing (Catalyst dev team)
  • Standards & docs — RFD 0006 marked published, plus /validate-dbt documentation polish (Catalyst dev team)

Keeping Your Plugin Up to Date — No reinstall needed. Cmd+Shift+P → “Developer: Reload Window”, or click the refresh icon in the Agent Plugins panel.

Share Your Feedback — Type catalyst-feedback in Copilot Chat to share what worked, what didn’t, or what could be better. The more specific you are (“expected X, got Y”), the faster we can act — and it now lands in a tracked ticket.
