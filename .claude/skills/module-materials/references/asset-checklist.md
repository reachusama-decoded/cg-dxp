## The lesson plan's timeline (the whole day)

The timeline always covers the full session, 0:00 to 4:00, in rows of 2.5 to 20 minutes in 2.5-minute steps (user, 2026-10-27), so the instructor sees the whole day. The start and end take 30 minutes on the template (the 15-minute intro, the closing debrief and the wrap-up; user, 2026-10-27); every module, with all its activities, is one hour (demo about 20, one row per demo `### ` heading, a note and a Say for each; challenge 20 to 25 in two rows; debrief 5; break 5 to 10); what's left is optional open challenge time, where learners carry on with any module challenge or take Put it together, the stretch for fast finishers. S01: welcome 0:00–0:15 (Meet and greet, Jordan and the day, What you will be able to do); Module 1 0:15–1:15, Module 2 1:15–2:15, Module 3 2:15–3:15, each demo in 2.5-minute rows, one per heading, Questioning 2.5, challenge 15 + 7.5 with a 2.5 Questioning row between, debrief 5, break 10; open challenge time 3:15–3:45 (20 + 10); closing debrief 3:45–3:50; wrap-up 3:50–4:00. A module not yet built keeps its standard rows there, marked "Written with the Module N handbook", and has no section or stub anywhere else (overview, anchors). The blank plan is the `lesson-plan` skill's `assets/lesson-plan-template.md`.
