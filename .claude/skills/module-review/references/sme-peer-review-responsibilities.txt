	SME Role Purpose
As the Subject Matter Expert, your role is to ensure the technical accuracy, clarity, and overall quality of the learning materials. Please complete each step thoroughly.
Role Responsibilities
Verify technical accuracy
* Carefully check that all concepts, explanations, workflows, and claims are technically correct.
* Do not skim, if anything is unclear or feels unfamiliar, pause and verify it through reliable sources.
* Confirm that terminology is used consistently and appropriately.
* Ensure examples align with industry best practice and reflect real-world usage.
Execute all code examples
* Run every code block in the intended learner environment
* Confirm:
   * All code executes without errors
   * Output matches what the lesson describes
   * Dependencies, data-access paths, and libraries are correct and up to date
* Flag any code that is fragile, unclear, overly complex or inconsistent with what learners have been taught so far.
Check pedagogical clarity
* Ensure instructions are clear, concise, and logically sequenced.
* Make sure explanations match the learners' expected skill level.
* Identify any gaps where additional guidance, examples or context may be needed.
* Highlight opportunities to simplify text, improve flow or reduce cognitive load.
Review spelling, grammar and readability
* Correct spelling, punctuation, grammar and formatting errors.
* Ensure consistent use of terminology, capitalisation and style.
* Improve readability where needed (e.g., shorter sentences, clearer headings, better variable names).
Validate alignment with lesson plan and learning outcomes
* Confirm that content, examples, and challenges align with the lesson plan learning objectives and intended learning journey.
* Ensure tasks are achievable with the skills learners have at that stage of the programme.
* Check for unnecessary complexity or missing prerequisite knowledge.
Identify risks and potential learner pitfalls
* Highlight areas where learners are likely to get confused or stuck.
* Suggest where additional hints, warnings, or notes might help.
* Flag any data issues, ambiguity, or assumptions that need clarifying.
Recommend improvements
* Suggest enhancements to examples, diagrams, challenges, or explanations.
* Propose alternative approaches if something could be taught more effectively.
* Identify opportunities to align more closely with best practice in data science/ML engineering.
As the SME, your review is essential for ensuring technical fidelity and learner success. Please capture any feedback, uncertainties, or revision requests clearly so the content team can action them efficiently.
