Keep brief notes during each phase, then finish them at 15:15. Link saved files using relative paths. Use [the evidence checklist](../docs/EVIDENCE.md) to check coverage.

## Consumer question and design
Who uses your output, and what can they answer? Which contract decisions changed during the day?

## My contribution
Name your domain/additional responsibility and both individual advanced components. Link your authored commits, reviews and the tested phase-2 pair integration commit. Identify supplied support and AI assistance.

## Information found
Give at least two findings from the real sources. For each, cite the product/source record, value or gap, and what the analyst can conclude. Label synthetic temporal results separately.

## Results and evidence
Link your source trace, ID/value checks, baseline/failure/repair results, workflow run IDs and accepted-output comparison. Explain any disagreement between expected and actual results.

## What was easy and what was hard
Describe one concrete example of each. What did you try, and how did a tool, collaborator or Catalyst help?

## My AI skill
Link the actual use and revised instructions. Which claim did you correct, and which source or executed check supported that correction?

## Advanced work
Explain your authored change and both clocks. Link macro/test and blueprint evidence and your individual temporal results. State which connected checks completed and which did not.

## Remaining gaps
List unfinished requirements, unsupported claims and the next action needed for each. Prepare your one-minute presentation from your contribution, one finding, a hard/easy reflection and an evidence link or limitation.
