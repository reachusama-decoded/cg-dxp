# price_sheet (Day 4)

Day 3's finished price sheet, as the starting point for Day 4's bitemporal section. `start/` holds the finished
files and `bash reset.sh` restores them. The anchor notebook then adds `models/history/fx_rates_history.sql` and
`macros/bitemporal_load.sql` and loads the three FX deliveries in `seeds/fx_deliveries.csv`: a first day, a second
day with a wrong GBP rate, and the vendor's correction. Generated by `research/scripts/build_day4_price_sheet.py`.
