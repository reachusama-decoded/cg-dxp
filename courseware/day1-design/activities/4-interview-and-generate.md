# Activity 4 — Interview and generate

*Day 1 · Threes · 30 minutes*

Choose a product you know. One of you is the producer (knows the upstream source and its constraints), one the consumer (knows the decision, the desired result and how they work today), one the creator (interviews both and reconciles them). Record the conversation, then give the transcript to Catalyst: generate a PRD, then a data contract. Inspect and correct both.

**Produce**

- A recorded, transcribed interview (Voice Memos on a Mac: record, pause, done, Transcript tab, copy; or a Teams meeting transcript)
- A PRD generated from the transcript
- A data contract generated after it
- Two corrections you made and why

**Finished early?** Ask Catalyst to list every field, owner and date in the contract that did not come from the transcript.

**Debrief questions**

- Which identifiers, keys, fields or definitions were wrong or missing?
- Where did it substitute generic knowledge for your internal source?
- Did the interview leave scope open or lead to an unsuitable table?
- What authoritative context fixed it?

**The seven questions the creator asks** (add your own):

1. What decision do you make with the data?
2. How do you make that decision today?
3. Who uses the result directly?
4. How is the source generated?
5. What do the terms mean in your work?
6. How do you recognise a usable result?
7. When do you need the result?

**Timing.** About five minutes to choose the product and roles, fifteen for the interview, ten to generate and inspect.

**Prompts.** `/ai-catalyst product-requirements-document` then paste the transcript. Then `/ai-catalyst data-contract` in the same conversation so it can read the PRD.
