# Activity 2 — Justify a product

*Day 1 · Tables · 20 minutes*

Pick one product from your list, or propose a new one for the firm. Answer the non-technical questions below; leave 'how would we build it' for later in the week. Note the question that was hardest, most surprising or most interesting.

**Produce**

- Who needs it, and what business problem or decision it supports
- Whether something like it exists, and what people do today
- Whether it can be built, and what would make that hard
- Whether the benefit is worth the development and ongoing cost
- Why consumers would adopt it, and why it should be prioritised now
- One unresolved question

**Finished early?** Take a second product, or test your first against a sceptical business owner: what would they say no to?

**Debrief questions**

- What changes for the person using this?
- Who is the business owner, by role?
- Which question could the table not answer, and who could?
